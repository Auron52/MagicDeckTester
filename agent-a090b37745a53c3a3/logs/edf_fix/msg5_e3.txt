fix(edf): the COMBO OFF route deploys onto a loop already in play (MTG_EDF_ROUTE_BOARD_DEPLOY)

With an outlet and a payload on the battlefield, comboroute::Run went straight to Loop, which casts only
from the float -- so the reducer, the land Auras or a land drop in hand, the cards that turn a
mana-neutral loop into a net-positive one, were never paid for off the lands. A Displacer blinking a
Cloud of Faeries pays {2}{C} against a two-land refund, banks nothing and stalls ("0 deploys, 61 blinks
without a step firing", EDF gi 1408 t5 with Training Grounds in hand; gi 547 t5 the same shape).

One lever, heurarm slot EDF_ROUTE_BOARD_DEPLOY (appended), default ON; =0 byte-identical.
 * Run: when the loop as it stands does not kill, retry after DeployPieces (land drop, Auras on the best
   host, a refund-covered untapper, the reducer) in both Aura orders; keep a deploy only when that trial
   KILLS. A trial-verified win, so it can turn a stalled route into a kill and never displaces one; the
   attempt index (-1 the board loop, 0/1 a deploy order) is what the live apply replays.
 * Ctx::protect_blink (deploy attempts only): the finish step no longer spends the float the next blink
   needs when every land is tapped, unless the float finishes the kill outright -- a deploy pays from the
   lands, so the loop can start with all of them tapped.
 * The trial memo key folds the lever (thread_local, outlives a batch job switch: without it a pooled
   A/B replays one arm's verdict in the other's paired games).

Unit: test/unit/test_edf_route_board_deploy.cpp (Displacer + Cloud + Essence Depleter in play, Training
Grounds in hand: off stalls, on kills and the live apply realises it; nothing to deploy -> unchanged).
