fix(attack-proj): Mana Cannons' cast triggers in the this-turn projection

MTG_ATTACK_CANNONS_TRIGGER (default ON; =0 not projected). Mana Cannons ("whenever you cast a
multicolored spell, deal X damage, X = its colour count"; FireOnCastTriggers) was in neither
consider() site, so a Cannons + five-colour-body turn never read as the lethal it was. Both sites now
add, per multicoloured cast of the plan, its colour count times the Cannons on our battlefield, walking
the casts in the order the apply worlds cast them (PlanCastOrder: CastOrderLess + the deck's set
arrangement) so a Cannons cast earlier in the same plan counts for the casts after it and never for
itself. Direct damage, so it counts in either main. Inert unless a Cannons is on our battlefield or
castable and a multicoloured cast is on offer.
