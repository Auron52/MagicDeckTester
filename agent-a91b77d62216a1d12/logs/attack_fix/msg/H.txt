fix(solve): the attack-only plan competes as a win on a lethal board

MTG_SOLVE_ATTACK_ONLY_WIN (default ON; =0 not scored). Solve's walk skips the empty selection, so
without a provider's opt-in board-lethal cut `best` started as the do-nothing plan with
wins_this_turn = false whatever the board's attack was. On a lethal board where every castable plan
taps an attacker -- so none projects a win once the attack-tap discount is honest -- d0 then cast the
best-eval spell and lost the lethal. The attack-only subset is now seeded through the same consider()
(guard pending_atk >= life), with NO early return and with its tie-break mask set past every real
subset: whenever a cast plan wins too the choice is exactly the walk's as before; only turns where
nothing else wins now attack instead. Not a greedy pick -- it scores one more plan with the projection
every plan gets.

Snow d0 gi1384 (seed 1551385) T5: after Heart + the Sheets look the re-solve's only castable was the
Frost Augur look, projected 10 vs 11 (it taps Augur and the Druid paying its {S}); the attack-only
line (11) was never scored. With MTG_ATTACK_TAP_ASTROLABE_CREDIT: 6 -> 5 (old 5). SelesnyaLifegain
d0 gi1094: 7 -> 6 (old 6).
