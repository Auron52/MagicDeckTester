fix(solve): a cast restrictor's subset must be realisable in some order (MTG_RESTRICTOR_ORDER_EXACT)

The d0-runner stall (Hinata2 2HG gi 254, d0 gi 195 / 865 / 1773). Both subset walks count the casts
after Irencrag Feat ("you can cast only one more spell this turn") by CastOrderRank and judge payability
order-free, crediting his seven red to the whole subset, his own cost included. Two failures follow:
  * 2HG gi 254 / d0 gi 195: {Irencrag, Crackle} whose {R}{R}{R} only his own float pays (Sol Ring + Izzet
    Boilerworks + Forbidden Orchard make two red) was committed and failed at payment turn after turn --
    nothing cast T3-T6 (17 failed casts);
  * d0 gi 865 / 1773: {Irencrag, Ornithopter, Crackle} on four mana is payable only Irencrag-first, so the
    executor's payable-order fallback cast him first, the Ornithopter took his one remaining cast and the
    Crackle was refused.
Neither is a USER-owned decision (payability/legality modelling, not cast order or policy).

RestrictorPrePayable, in both walks right after the existing casts-after test, REJECT-ONLY (`after` is
untouched, so the USER's Irencrag waste gate keeps judging the rank order and never readmits a subset):
  (1) pre-set: the casts ranked at or before the restrictor + the restrictor, paid WITHOUT its float? If
      not, one more cast follows it -> rejected when that breaks the cap;
  (2) self: the restrictor alone from the board + every other producer, any order? If not, rejected.
Bill = the subset's credited bill minus every non-pre action; supply = board + pre-pass / pre-ranked
producers; colours = the walks' own ColorFeasibility on the pre-set -- all generous, so a payable line is
never rejected. Rank-ordered decks only (not OrderingSearchEnabled, i.e. not Dragonstorm). heurarm slot
RESTRICTOR_ORDER_EXACT appended; DEFAULT ON; =0 -> the rank-only count.
