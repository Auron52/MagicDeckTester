fix(dragonstorm): breakpoint leftovers are ordered with the continuation, not cast after it (MTG_DRAGONSTORM_BP_LEFTOVERS)

USER: "after the breakpoint the cast order should follow my order" -- Dragonstorm last. When an Apex of
Power breakpoint interrupted a cast list (the main plan or a continuation), the list's REMAINING casts
were made after the continuation the breakpoint re-solved: after its Dragonstorm (d0 s7000158 T8:
Scourge cast after the storm, so it pinged once instead of per storm Dragon and missed the storm count),
or Irencrag after the continuation's Utvara, its seven red wasted (suite-A/B game s1191413 T3).

Design: the leftovers JOIN the continuation. MergeBreakpointLeftovers (ManaPayment) orders the union
by the deck's order (comparator + ArrangeCastOrder + payable fallback) and keeps it when no
continuation cast loses its payment (ExactOrderPaidMask -- the exact walk run the way the executor runs
a list, skipping what it cannot pay). Otherwise the leftovers are CARRIED after the continuation's own
casts: a later breakpoint inside it (a second Apex) takes them as its own leftovers and orders them
again with the mana its exile brings (s7000158: Scourge cannot precede Dragonstorm on the first exile's
mana but can on the second's); with none they are cast after, as before (the nearest order that pays).
Either way the continuation is replayed as an explicit order and the interrupted list stops there.
Chosen over "leftovers first" (it would put a leftover Irencrag/Dragonstorm in front of the exiled
rituals and cap the continuation) and over "drop them" (the d0 second pass then casts them after the
storm anyway, and a searched line would lose them).

Lockstep: the executor merges in resolve_draw_breakpoint at the point ApplyPlanDirect's impulse-exile
branch merges (after the continuation's land and pre-casts); the executor's lists stop with
bp_trunc_exec, the rollout's with bp_truncate; a committed line replays the merged recorded segment.
Provider-owned opt-in (DecisionProvider::MergesBreakpointLeftovers): Dragonstorm, an Apex site, USER
order on. Lever MTG_DRAGONSTORM_BP_LEFTOVERS (heurarm slot appended), default ON; =0 byte-identical to
f1cd15e4 (27 jobs / 540 games). Sanity A/B (paired, seeds 7900001+): d0 300: 0 turns (0 faster, 0
slower); d3 100: 0 (search units 318557 vs 318565). s7000158 T8 now casts Seething, Scourge,
Dragonstorm; s1191413 T3 Apex, Irencrag, Utvara -- same win turns.
Unit: test_dragonstorm_followups.cpp (merge in order, carry, dedupe, Irencrag before a lone Dragon).
