fix(combat): a non-lethal pump keeps the depletion / storage lands' mana (MTG_FB_HOLD_BANKED)

ApplyFirebreathing spends ALL leftover mana on attacker pumps (Scourge {R}, Lathliss {1}{R}) on the
premise that combat is the turn's last mana use, so unspent mana is lost. That is false for a BANKED
source: a depletion land (Sandstone Needle: a counter per tap, sacrificed at zero) and a storage land
(Mercadian Bazaar, Dwarven Hold: counters banked over turns, +1 more for each turn left untapped).

A pump now holds those sources back unless the full pump makes the attack lethal. Lethality is judged
on a copy -- the full pump with the caller's own payer, then the shared ResolveCombatDamage. One helper
in Combat.cpp (FbHoldBankedThisCombat, FbBankedHoldScope, FbPumpPayHold), called from both pump sites
(AIEngine::Firebreathe and the rollout's SimulateCombat), so executor and rollout stay in lockstep. The
pump payer re-installs the hold per payment, so a pay-sac erase cannot misdirect it. The human-chooser
path is left to the human.

Named games (dragonstorm d0): s1191687 gi1686 7 -> 6 (the ab3 old arm's 6; the Needle was sacrificed
to a non-lethal T3 pump) and s7600233 gi232 7 -> 6 (the Bazaar's bank).

Lever: MTG_FB_HOLD_BANKED (default ON) + heurarm slot FB_HOLD_BANKED, appended after
DRAGONSTORM_IRENCRAG_CLOSER; shared reader FbHoldBankedOn (EngineFlags.h).
Unit test: test_dragonstorm_followups.cpp "Combat pump keeps a depletion / storage land's mana ..."
(Needle and Bazaar, non-lethal and lethal, both arms).
Built and tested together with the next commit (MTG_LINE_FLOAT_TAP_FIRST), one build for both.
