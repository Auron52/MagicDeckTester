fix(pay): pay a generic pip off a spare source before floating colour the line still owes (MTG_LINE_FLOAT_TAP_FIRST)

The shared payer spends the turn-scoped reserve (state.floating_mana) before it taps anything,
generic pips included (SpendFloatingTowardCost). dragonstorm d3 s7100081 T2: Mountain + Unclaimed
Territory, Rite of Flame floats {R}{R}, Ruby Medallion's {2} spent BOTH, and Pyretic Ritual's {R} was
left to the Territory, which makes only {C} for a noncreature spell. The line "Rite, Medallion, Pyretic,
Seething, Lathliss" pays with the Territory's {C} on the Medallion (the exact walk says so) but lost
three casts.

New first rung in TapForCostSharedImpl, the autonomous twin of the human-only MTG_PAY_LINE_FLOAT_HOLD.
When the reserve would pay generic pips out of colours the rest of the applying line still owes
(g_line_unpaid_cost minus this cost's own pips), one held attempt keeps k of those generic pips off the
reserve and reserves every NON-spare source, so a spare one pays them:
  * spare = a land or rock whose payment-legal colours include no colour a later pip owes; never a dork,
    a depletion / storage land or a pay-sac source;
  * a creature-only-colour land (Territory, Cavern) is spare for a noncreature payment only if every
    colour the line owes that it could make for a later creature is also on offer from the reserve or
    another unrestricted untapped source (the s7100151 shape: the Territory was Kolaghan's only {B});
  * k = min(that owed generic, the spare yield); a later {C} pip in the line disables it.
Any failure falls through to the unchanged sequence. Off in human play. Executor, rollout and the
payability walks share the payer, so they stay in lockstep.

Named game: s7100081 (d3 b10). With the lever on the search no longer commits the failing T2 line (it
now prices it right) and casts the Medallion alone; win turn 4 on both arms. The T2 board itself is the
unit test.

Lever: MTG_LINE_FLOAT_TAP_FIRST (default ON) + heurarm slot LINE_FLOAT_TAP_FIRST, appended after
FB_HOLD_BANKED; shared reader LineFloatTapFirstOn (EngineFlags.h).
Unit test: test_dragonstorm_followups.cpp "Line float: a spare source pays the generic ..." (the T2
board on both arms, the {B} guard, no live line).

Both new levers =0: byte-identical to b7cfa4f1 on 36 jobs (720 games: Dragonstorm d0 / d3 / shipped,
Hinata2 d0 / d3, Mirrorwing d0 / d3, Dragons d0 / d3, Giants, Knights, Minotaur, Angels, Prevent Damage,
Cyclone d0). Unit binary: 938 / 938 pass.
