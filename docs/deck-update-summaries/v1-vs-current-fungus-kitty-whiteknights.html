A goldfish win on turn 6 or later (≥T6, including non-wins) is too slow against a fast deck.

  • Fungus at 2HG is the largest gain measured on any deck — nearly a full turn. v1 won by turn 4 in 2 games out of 50,000 there; the current list does it in a third of games, at a seventh of the cost.
  • Kitty Equipment is where the mean misleads: its −0.44t looks middling, but turn-3 kills go from 13% to 51% of standard games.
  • All three gain more at 2HG. For White Knights that is one card, Adeline, which makes a token per opponent.

Method. 1.8M games (100k standard / 50k 2HG per arm for Fungus and Kitty, 500k per arm for White Knights), depth 7, budget 200 ms, max_turns 8. 2HG = 30 life, 2 opponent heads. 10 paired blocks per arm: both lists see the same shuffles. Each list plays with its own shipped profile and keep table.
