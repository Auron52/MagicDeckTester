A goldfish win on turn 6 or later (≥T6, including non-wins) is too slow against a fast deck.

  • Goldfish measures speed only. All three decks also have ways to slow the opponent down (lifegain, removal and other answers), and the speed gains came without giving up much of that.
  • Angels is the largest gain: games running to turn 6 or later fall from 27% to 6.6% at standard and from 55% to 20.4% at 2HG. v1 was already a good deck in real games, because its lifegain and removal push the game long; the new list mainly helps against fast combo, where v1 needed the right removal at the right time.
  • Stompy now wins on turn 3 (0.50% → 8.84% of standard games) and is 2.3–2.6× cheaper to play.
  • The format reorders the gains: Stompy gains 36% more at 2HG, Critter slightly more, and Angels is the only deck whose edge narrows.

Method. 2.4M games (500k per arm per format for Angels and Critter, 100k for Stompy), depth 7, budget 200 ms, max_turns 8. 2HG = 30 life, 2 opponent heads. 10 paired blocks per arm: both lists see the same shuffles. Each list plays with its own shipped profile and keep table.
