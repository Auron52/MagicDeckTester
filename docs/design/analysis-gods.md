# Gods deck analysis

## Deck and scope

- Source: `decks/Gods/Gods.cod` (untracked when analysis began).
- Main deck: 60 cards across 30 distinct names. The seven sideboard Gods are unreachable in the goldfish simulator because the main deck has no wish effect; they are outside this run.
- Intended line appears to use dredge and loot effects to fill the graveyard, then Replenish to return God enchantments. This is a working hypothesis pending play verification.

- Shared search regression found and fixed (2026-11-07): the `unclaimed_tap_ordering` Dragonstorm scenario failed after the Gods work while clean HEAD passed. Raw action collection still offered all seven hand casts, but the plan odometer silently rejected their mixed subsets. `FoldPrefixBitsViolated` and `FoldPrefixBitsViolatedSelected` split a packed 64-bit mask into two independent 32-bit equivalence classes; the Gods-related width sweep had accidentally widened each half check to 64 bits, so a bit in the upper class contaminated the lower prefix test. Restoring 32-bit casts made the original scenario pass at turn five. The temporary action/plan diagnostics were removed. The 430-scenario preflight then passed 430/430. Focused Gods tests pass 14 cases/169 assertions with the experimental flags on and 14 cases/145 assertions with them off.
- Smoke regression after the fold repair (2026-11-07): the batch completed 176 configurations in 208 seconds, with 150 exact matches and 26 digest/average mismatches. Four searched games got slower, two faster; 70 searched games changed play at the same score. The four slower cases are Fluctuator 2HG gi1 and gi55, Fluctuator gi1, and Treasure Hunt 2HG gi35. The automatic replay explanations failed with `Unknown keyword: Landwalk`, so these are **unclassified regressions**, not an accepted rebaseline. D0 had four slower games. Full output: `build/scratch/gods-smoke-regression-fixed.log` (ignored diagnostic). This gate remains open, separate from Gods' unsolved memory/profile gate.
- Gods heuristic experiments (2026-11-07 continuation): Three default-off provider switches are available: `MTG_GODS_DREDGE_RULE=1` offers ordinary draw plus the highest-mill physical dredger (Grave-Troll before Imp before Loam), and also Loam when fewer than three lands are in play and a land is in the graveyard; `MTG_GODS_DISCARD_RULE=1` ranks Grave-Troll, Imp, Loam, then Gods for looting and cleanup discards, with one candidate for autonomous search; `MTG_GODS_MAGUS_UPKEEP=1` (paired with the discard rule) activates ready Magi in upkeep before the draw step and omits their main-phase autonomous action. `MTG_UNPRUNED` bypasses all three. Human main-phase decisions remain fully surfaced, but the new upkeep activation currently has no human decision prompt and automatically uses every ready Magus. This timing rule remains an experiment, not a viewer-ready adoption. On seed 92001 at d1/b10/max-turn4, dredge+discard finished in 48.72 s and 282,164 KiB peak RSS, unwon at the four-turn horizon. Either switch alone exceeded 90 s (dredge-only peak 883,424 KiB; discard-only 703,964 KiB); the old full-choice arm exceeded 60 s. At max-turn5, dredge+discard won on turn five in 126.53 s/421,348 KiB; adding upkeep Magus preserved the turn-five win and finished in 72.34 s/401,060 KiB. These are single-seed cost signals, not quality validation. A ten-game full-choice max-turn3 batch completed only three unwon games in 75 s, and that horizon is too early to compare wins. Keep all switches default off pending outcome-valid oracle comparison and viewer upkeep choice implementation. Gods test runs use `MTG_RSS_CAP_GB=20` per the user's preferred ceiling; the 17.6 GiB host-derived default is unchanged for other work.
- Dredge-rule variant sweep (2026-11-07): `MTG_GODS_DREDGE_RULE=1` is the earlier two-choice policy. Variant `2` made a single state-conditional choice (highest dredger, Loam when land-starved, normal draw for land/payoff), cutting seed 92001/92003/92007 d1/b10/max-turn5 to under a second and below 45 MiB RSS, but it LOST the turn-five wins on 92001 and 92007; reject it on quality. Variant `3` widened only when land or Replenish was uncertain; it restored those wins but 92007 still used 13.5 GiB RSS. Variant `4` chooses exactly one: Loam with fewer than three lands in play, no land in hand, and a graveyard land; normal draw with fewer than two lands and no hand land, or with at least three lands plus three graveyard Gods and no Replenish in hand; otherwise the highest dredger. With discard and upkeep Magus flags also on, variant 4 was no slower on any of nine paired depth-1/max-turn5 games against variant 1 and improved 92002 from turn five to turn four. The exact five-game seed-92005 batch went from 86.15 s/14,197,164 KiB (variant 1) to 1.04 s/42,584 KiB (variant 4), both 5.8 loss-penalized average. These are nine paired games at a short horizon, not a regression or oracle proof. The 13.5 GiB variant-1 peak reproduces in a single 92007 game; at an 8 GiB diagnostic cap the bounded FSL and plan caches were zero, and a gdb stop placed the worker in a mulligan rollout's `AppendSubdecisionAxes` path. Variant 4 d3/b10/max-turn5 seed 92001 won turn five in 1.82 s/72,744 KiB. A d5/b20/full-eight-turn game with the same seed and variant 4 exceeded the requested 20 GiB cap at 139.66 s with only 17 MiB in the bounded plan cache; a gdb diagnostic showed nested breakpoint continuation enumeration during a mulligan rollout. Therefore the full-horizon depth-5 baseline/profile remains blocked by transient search memory even under the strongest sampled rule. None of these experimental policies is adopted by default.
