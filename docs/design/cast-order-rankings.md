# Cast-order rankings, per deck

Generated by `mtg <deck> --cast-order-report` with `MTG_IDEAL_ORDER=1`, i.e. the ranks the
engine actually uses (`DecisionProvider::CastOrderRank`), not a re-implementation. Lower rank
= cast earlier. See docs/design/cast-order-ideal-with-ranges.md.


## What to check, and the three I already doubt

The draw tier (rank 2) is `IsIdealOrderDraw` -- deliberately the SAME card classes `OrderingOpaque`
refuses to order today (draw / cast_draw / cast_reorder / staging / EI / impulse / cascade / retrace
/ solo-target trick), so the two can be compared like for like. That predicate keys on "does it draw",
which is right for a cantrip and wrong for a big spell that happens to draw. Three land in the draw
tier that look mis-tiered as an ORDER statement:

| card | mv | deck | why it looks wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Throes of Chaos | 4 | Unpredictable Cyclone | cascade payoff, not information-gathering |
| Magma Opus | 8 | Hinata2 | the deck's payoff; "cast it first" is not a real instruction |
| Apex of Power | 10 | Dragonstorm | ditto, and it is the whole go-off |

The RANGE is the designed answer to exactly this -- an 8-mana draw cannot be cast early anyway, so
the ladder pushes it down to where it is payable -- but a rank that says "first" for a 10-drop is
misleading on its face, and it may be better to gate the draw tier on cheapness (a cantrip) and let
the big draws keep their natural tier. That is a judgement call, which is why it is here rather than
decided.

Everything else in tier 2 is a genuine cantrip (Ponder, Preordain, Expressive Iteration, Light Up
the Stage, Treasure Hunt, Gold Rush's trick payload).
