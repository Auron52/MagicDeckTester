# The integration's final held-out A/B (2026-10-13) and its slower games

Status: A/B DONE; smoke / regression tiers in progress (local only; the USER is holding all pushes). This records the per-deck bar for the
discard-policy / bug-fix integration (rebased onto origin 27c5976d), every slower game's verdict, and
the fixes made on the way.

## Method

* **Arms.** `new` = the tip's defaults. `off` = every default-ON lever added since origin turned off
  (`logs/discard_impl/lever_defaults.py`; 68 levers at 6f32f1e5, 70 at 179d8275, 72 with the two below).
  The identity checks (origin vs the tip with every lever =0: 68/68 jobs byte-identical) make `off` the
  committed tree.
* **Cells, per deck** (`logs/discard_impl/make_ab5.py`): the shipped cell (the profile's value_play depth,
  `--budget-ms 20`) 200 games, d3 b10 300, d0 2000. Seeds `6000001 + 20000*deck + 5000*cell` (disjoint
  from every earlier A/B). Paired per game; a loss scores 9; net = sum(new - off).
* **Bar** (CLAUDE.md): every DECK's net <= 0, and every slower game recovers -- stage 1 at
  `--depth W --budget-ms 100` (W = the off arm's win turn), stage 2 at `--depth 8 --budget-ms 0`
  (`logs/discard_impl/recover_ab5.py`). A game that recovers at neither is bisected one lever at a time
  (`bisect_ab5.py`) and root-caused.
* Batches: ab5 = 30 decks on 6f32f1e5; ab5b = EDF, Bruna, Fungus, Fungus-b on 179d8275 (the two EDF
  levers picked after ab5 started touch only those decks); ab6ds = Dragonstorm on the fixed binary.

## Slower games that did not recover, and what was done

### Dragonstorm: `MTG_FB_HOLD_BANKED` lost the Scourge race (fixed: `MTG_FB_HOLD_BANKED_NEXT_TURN`)

d3 gi 60 / 70, shipped gi 69 / 70. The combat pump held Sandstone Needle's mana unless the pump was lethal
THIS combat. In a Scourge race that costs a turn: pumping the Needle on T3 kills on T4, holding it kills
on T5. The pump is the permitted greedy combat exemption, not searched, so no depth recovers it.
The fix projects both worlds one turn on (the board untapped, an idle storage land's +1, the depletion
sweep, next turn's clairvoyant draw and its land drop, the same attackers with the full pump) and spends
the bank only when that alone buys a next-turn kill. Two first drafts were wrong in instructive ways:
without the depletion sweep the sacrificed Needle paid again in the projection (s1191687 gi 1686 went
6 -> 7, the very game the hold was written for); without the land drop the projection was a land short
(shipped gi 70 stayed at 6). With both: all four games back to the committed tree's turn, the two named
games of the original lever still 6.

### Dragonstorm: the payoff prune deleted "Rite of Flame -> Ruby Medallion" (fixed: `MTG_PAYOFF_PRUNE_REDUCER_SINK`)

d3 gi 289 (5 vs 3), shipped gi 188 (8 vs 7); the one-lever bisect names `MTG_DRAGONSTORM_USER_ORDER`, but
the USER's order is not the defect. The payoff prune (dragonstorm-payoff-prune.md) drops any subset that
casts a ritual without a Dragon, Dragonstorm or Apex, so "Rite of Flame -> Ruby Medallion now, storm next
turn" is unfindable at every budget. The committed tree reached it only through a PHANTOM plan --
{Rite, Medallion, Pyretic, Seething, Scourge} on one Mountain, whose casts after the Medallion all failed
at payment (`MTG_USER_ORDER_TRACE`: `paid=0`), leaving exactly Rite + Medallion. The USER order's exact
payable walk no longer commits such a plan, which exposed the prune. The fix counts a permanent spell-cost
reducer as the ritual's sink, in the SEARCH BRANCH LIST only: admitting it in the greedy policy too (the d0
runner, the rollouts) measured worse at d0 (fresh seeds, 2000 games: +68, 48 faster / 84 slower).

### Hinata shipped gi 137 (8 vs 7): the restrictor is right; a breakpoint-search gap underneath

Bisected to `MTG_RESTRICTOR_ORDER_EXACT`, but its rejects here are correct: `{Irencrag + Magma Opus}` and
`{Soulfire + Irencrag}` after Soulfire's breakpoint are payable only by Irencrag's own float. The committed tree
reached T7 because that phantom was `MTG_BP_BASE_CANON`'s single pick at a deferred breakpoint site; applying it
cast nothing, which left the lands untapped for the main-2 fixpoint's real line (Ponder -> Preordain -> Reality
Spasm -> Irencrag -> Crackle). With the phantom gone the pick is a real `{Magma Opus}` and "cast nothing more"
is never scored at that site at any budget. Root cause, prototype fix (`MTG_BP_EMPTY_ARM_DEFERRED`: gi 137 -> T7
at shipped and d8 b0; 200 paired Hinata games identical, +2.6-3.5% units) and what adoption needs:
docs/design/bp-empty-arm-deferred-site.md. Not on the branch.

### Dragonstorm d0 gi 580 / 700 / 1308: the committed tree's kills used an illegal splice

Each committed-tree kill casts a plain Desperate Ritual first and then splices the other copy, revealing the copy
that has just left the hand (MTG_USER_ORDER_TRACE: `Desperate Ritual ... splice=0` then `... splice=1`). The USER
order's splice legality refuses that line; at `--depth 8 --budget-ms 0` the legal optimum is T2 / T4 / T5 against
the committed tree's illegal T1 / T3 / T4. Not a regression.

### FiveColour d0 gi 1106: the projection never counted Maelstrom Archangel's free cast (fixed: `MTG_ATTACK_FREECAST_DAMAGE`)

No single lever turned on reproduces it; turning off either MTG_ATTACK_DOMAIN_DORK_YIELD or MTG_ATTACK_TAP_NEED_EXACT
restores T4. Both levers are right; the committed tree won T4 by a cancellation. T4 board: 9 mana (three lands,
Birds 1, Faeburrow Elder 5), Unite the Coalition costs 7, so casting it precombat taps Faeburrow; the Archangel's
combat damage then casts the second Unite free (10 more). Neither projection site priced that free cast. On the
tip {Unite} read 5 + 10 = 15 (not lethal) and the d0 runner took {Archangel #2, Faeburrow #2} on value -> T5; on
the committed tree Faeburrow's yield read 1, so it was not tapped and {Unite} read a false 10 + 10 = 20 "lethal" --
the phantom 5 stood in for the missing free 10. The fix (pre-combat only, both consider() sites and the d0
attack-only seed) adds the best hand spell's direct damage per untapped attacking free-caster, excluding X spells,
alt costs and anything MTG_FREECAST_PRUNE would drop. gi 1106 -> T4. Paired, FiveColour, lever on vs =0, seeds
9200001+: d0 1000 -10 (10 / 0), d3 b10 150 and shipped 100 identical.

At d8 b0 every arm (tip, either lever off, the fix) is T5: the searched game leaves this line at T2 -- with the
USER's FiveColour main-phase rules (MTG_5C_PHASE) Faeburrow is held to main 2 there and the main-2 enumeration
never offers it, so main 1 casts the Triome cycle instead; with MTG_5C_PHASE=0 d8 b0 finds T4 (Faeburrow T2,
Archangel T3, Unite T4). Not a lever regression; a USER-rule wiring question, surfaced.

Re-measured on the final A/B's seeds (ab6fc, b517017a): FiveColour -207 (206 / 4, every slower game recovers at
stage 1; gi 1106 now 4 = the committed tree); fresh seeds 8100001+ vs MTG_ATTACK_FREECAST_DAMAGE=0: d0 -35 (35 / 0),

### burn d3 gi 83 and Auras d0 gi 1634: the USER's discard rules (USER decisions; amendments measured, default off)

* burn: one land for five turns; on T5 the policy sheds a Shard Volley ("a Volley with no land of its own")
  and keeps a Searing Blaze that can deal only 1 (no land in hand -- the last entry of the USER's burn order).
  T6 draws a Mountain: the committed tree casts both Volleys and kills; the bucket policy is a Volley short.
* Auras: on T4 the policy sheds Alpha Authority as the worst power-per-mana Aura; its value counts the scaler
  credit of scalers on the board only, while the "blank" test already counts a scaler kept in hand (Ethereal
  Armor). By T6 every enchantment is +1 through the scalers; the committed tree casts Alpha Authority and swings
  24 for lethal, the bucket policy 19.
