How to read this
Win turn
The average turn the deck kills a goldfish in the table's simulated games. No kill by turn 8 counts as 9. Lower is better.
Keep or mulligan
Keep is this hand after the best bottom. Mulligan is the expected next hand, played the same way from there. The lower one is the engine's call.
Confidence
How many standard errors separate the two. Fewer games means more noise.
Clear2 or more≈98%+ that more games agree
Likely1 to 2≈84–98%
Marginalunder 1a close call
Goldfish only
The opponent sits at 20 life and never casts a spell, blocks or interacts, so removal, counterspells, blockers and life gain earn little. That matters only in close calls, so Marginal ones carry a reminder.
